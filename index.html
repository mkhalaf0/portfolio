01 — About

Low-power intelligent sensing
for human-centered environments

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Engineering at Northeastern University, working within the Spatial Intelligence Research Group (SINRG) under Prof. Mallesham Dasari. My research sits at the intersection of wearable computing, wireless sensing, and edge intelligence — building systems that perceive and reason about physical space with minimal power budgets.

I investigate multimodal traffic dynamics in XR and AI glasses, develop capacitive sensing approaches for eye tracking, and design adaptive streaming and bitrate control for immersive content. My work spans Bluetooth, BLE, Wi-Fi Direct, and 5G protocols, always aiming to push the boundary of what lightweight, wearable devices can sense and understand.

Before starting my Ph.D., I earned my B.S. in Electrical & Computer Engineering from Norwich University (with minors in Mathematics and Computer Science), where I was named Student Engineer of the Year and served as President of Eta Kappa Nu.

02 — Research Areas

Sensing the invisible
architecture of space

Wireless Sensing

Dissecting multimodal traffic dynamics across XR and AI glasses — measuring end-to-end latency, throughput, and power over Bluetooth Classic, BLE, Wi-Fi Direct, and 5G. Comparing real-world performance across Meta Ray-Ban, Dragon Glass, Even G1, and Brilliant Labs Frame.

Bluetooth/BLE Wi-Fi Direct 5G Wireshark

Wearable Spatial Inference

Designing capacitive sensor-based eye tracking for power-efficient gaze estimation in mobile XR. Building AI-powered smart glasses for visually impaired users to increase mobility and independence through spatial awareness.

Capacitive Sensing Eye Tracking XR Glasses Accessibility

Edge ML for Embedded Devices

Object-level dynamic bitrate control for live XR scene streaming using geometric mesh representations. Leveraging edge computation for adaptive streaming algorithms that balance quality-of-experience with bandwidth constraints in real-time.

Adaptive Bitrate Mesh Streaming QoE Optimization

IoT System Architecture

Designing networked system infrastructure for immersive multimedia — spanning cloud infrastructure, data pipelines, CI/CD automation, and observability for distributed systems. From hardware prototyping with ESP32 and Raspberry Pi to scalable cloud deployments.

Cloud/DevOps ESP32 Raspberry Pi Distributed Systems
